Welcome

You have come to a place with a very long memory. That is not said to intimidate you. It is said to invite you into something real.

Every community is a quilt made of the people who came before — their choices and their losses, their labor and their love for a particular bend in a particular river. Scottsville's quilt is old and specific and full of stories that deserve to be known.

You are here now. There is a square for you too — space genuinely set aside, not reluctantly but earnestly, because a living community needs new people the way a river needs rain.

This town notices things, and it has long eyes and a longer memory. But what it remembers isn't where you came from or how your name sounds in somebody else's mouth. It remembers whether you were here — in some way that mattered — or whether you were just present, the way furniture is present.

Scottsville meets people halfway. But you do have to walk that half.
