Museums & Libraries

The Scottsville Museum

The Scottsville Museum should be everyone's first stop in town, whether simply visiting or making this town your home. The museum preserves the area's history since before the town's founding and offers exhibits that help tell the story of Scottsville and its people. It also has an impressive archive, some of which is available on their website. Admission is free.

Hours: 10:00 am – 5:00 pm Saturday, April through October
1:00 pm – 5:00 pm Sunday, April through October
Also open Memorial Day, July 4th, and Labor Day, or by appointment.

290 Main Street, Scottsville, Virginia 24590
smuseum.avenue.org

Scottsville Public Library

An absolute gem in our little town, and the staff is exceptional. It offers free classes, workshops, children's activities, and so much more.

330 Bird Street #4390, Scottsville, VA 24590
(434) 286-3541
jmrl.org/scottsville

Fluvanna County Public Library

Offers many wonderful programs for children and adults. Scottsville residents who live in Fluvanna County can obtain a library card for free; those who live in Albemarle County can get one for a small annual fee. Regardless of your address, everyone is welcome to take advantage of their free programs and activities.

214 Commons Boulevard, Palmyra, VA 22963
(434) 589-1400
youseemore.com/fluvanna

Parks & Playgrounds

Dorrier Park

A clean, well-maintained park with a playground, a reservable picnic shelter, two tennis courts, a soccer field, a softball field, a trail, and restrooms. A great place to meet other parents, play some pickleball, or take the dog for a walk along the levee.

250 Page Street, Scottsville, VA 24590
albemarle.org — Dorrier Park

Simpson Park

A great park in the Esmont community with a playground, tennis courts, picnic tables, a splash pad, and a public restroom. Dogs are welcome.

2410 Simpson Lane, Esmont, VA 22937

Totier Creek Reservoir Park

A tranquil spot on the banks of the reservoir with a boat launch, picnic tables, grills, and restrooms. Dogs are welcome. A great place to take the kids fishing.

9290 Totier Creek Road, Scottsville, VA 24590

Canal Basin Square

An outdoor transportation history park on the site of the old James River and Kanawha Canal Turning Basin. It features replicas of the famous batteaux that transported goods along the James River, along with signs, maps, and other information on the history of the town and the importance of the river. Public restrooms available. Dogs are welcome.

Canal Basin Square is also the home of Scottsville's Fridays on the Canal festival, which runs throughout the summer months.

249 Main Street, Scottsville, VA 24590
cbs.avenue.org
Fridays on the Canal schedule: scottsvillefridaysonthecanal.com
